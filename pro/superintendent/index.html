Frequently asked questions

Marco Polo Pro includes all of the features of Plus, including speed controls (1.5 - 3x), background audio, unlimited storage of Polo video messages, transcripts, photo and voice-only Polos, and more. With Pro, we've added new features that are optimized for our business community.

Pro works for teams of varying sizes but we recommend Pro for teams of 10+. For those managing teams of five members or less, we recommend Plus Family, which includes six accounts on one billing plan.

We have construction teams of all sizes, from under 10 members to teams that number in the hundreds. Pro users span from general contractors to specialty trades, from project managers to field supervisors. The common denominator is remote and hybrid work environments where face-to-face communication improves project outcomes.
