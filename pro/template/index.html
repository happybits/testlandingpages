Yes, absolutely. Marco Polo Pro uses enterprise-grade security including 256-bit AES encryption, SOC 2 Type II compliance, and GDPR compliance. All video messages are encrypted in transit and at rest. We never store data on personal devices - everything is securely hosted on our servers with redundant backups. Your construction project data, including videos, transcripts, and files, are protected with the same security standards used by Fortune 500 companies.

Marco Polo Pro integrates with leading construction management platforms including Procore, PlanGrid, Buildertrend, and Autodesk Construction Cloud. You can share videos and transcripts directly to project files, link conversations to specific tasks, and embed communication into your existing workflows. We also offer API access for custom integrations with your internal systems. Our construction specialists can help configure integrations during onboarding.

Most construction teams are up and running within 15 minutes. Setup involves downloading the app, creating team groups for each project, and adding team members. We provide construction-specific onboarding including project templates, communication best practices, and integration setup. For larger teams (25+ members), we offer dedicated implementation support with a construction industry specialist to ensure smooth rollout across all your jobsites.

Marco Polo Pro is designed for construction environments with limited connectivity. Videos automatically compress for low-bandwidth situations, and the app queues messages to send when connection improves. You can record videos offline and they'll upload when you're back in coverage. We also support offline viewing of previously downloaded messages. Many teams use jobsite Wi-Fi or mobile hotspots to ensure consistent connectivity for their most important project communications.
